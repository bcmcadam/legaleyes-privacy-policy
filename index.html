LegalEyes Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

1. Information We Collect

LegalEyes collects the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication data through Firebase Auth
  • Document Data: Legal documents you upload for analysis
  • Usage Data: App usage statistics and crash reports
  • Payment Information: Subscription and purchase data through Apple's App Store

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and analyze your legal documents using AI technology
  • Provide document summaries and risk analysis
  • Manage your subscription and account
  • Improve our services and user experience

3. AI Processing

LegalEyes uses OpenAI's API to analyze your documents. Document content is processed securely and is not stored by OpenAI beyond the processing session.

4. Data Storage

  • Firebase Services: We use Google Firebase for authentication, document storage, and database services
  • Security: All data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Location: Data is stored securely in Google Cloud infrastructure

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our rights and safety

6. Data Retention

  • Documents: Automatically deleted after 30 days unless saved
  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Payment Data: Handled by Apple, not stored by us

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption
  • Biometric authentication support
  • Secure data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Regular security audits

9. Children's Privacy

LegalEyes is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
