🔧 Common Issues

App Won't Load Documents

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure document is a supported format (PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT)
  • Try restarting the app

Sign In Problems

  • Check your email and password
  • Try "Forgot Password" to reset
  • Ensure you have an internet connection

Subscription Issues

  • Subscriptions are managed through the App Store
  • Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions
  • Find LegalEyes to manage or cancel

Document Processing Slow

  • Large documents take longer to process
  • Check your internet connection speed
  • Premium users get priority processing

💡 How to Use LegalEyes

Upload a Document

  1. Tap the "+" button on home screen
  2. Choose Camera, Files, or Photos
  3. Select your legal document
  4. Wait for AI analysis to complete

Understanding Results

  • Summary: Plain English overview
  • Red Flags: Important risks to review
  • Key Clauses: Important terms explained
